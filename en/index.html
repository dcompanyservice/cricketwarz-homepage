How a match works

Short real-time matches, solo or with a teammate. You make the choices; your players' abilities do the rest.

1

Build your team

Collect cartoon cricketers and set your batting order.

2

Bat

Aim each shot. Where you aim and who is batting decide how far the ball goes.

3

Bowl

Pick a line for each ball and try to beat the batter.

4

Play anyone

Join as a solo team or a 2-player team. Solo and 2-player teams are matched together, so 1v1, 1v2 and 2v2 can all happen. If nobody is found within 8 seconds, a bot of a similar level fills in.

Features

  • Real-time matchesPlay as a solo team or share a team with a teammate. Two grounds: the Stadium with standard rules, and the Gully with street-cricket house rules.
  • ClubsCreate or join a club, play club battles and climb the monthly club ranking. Season rewards arrive in your in-game mailbox.
  • Collect charactersDraw cartoon cricketers and their shards. The odds are shown in the game before every draw.
  • Grow your teamLevel up your characters and use shards to raise their stars.
  • Free to playFree to download and play. Optional purchases: a premium pass, gem packs and a one-time growth pack. Ads play only when you choose to watch one for a reward.
