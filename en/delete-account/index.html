Delete your Cricketwarz account

Cricketwarz · Developer: Dcompany (dcompanyservice)

You can ask us to delete your Cricketwarz account and the data linked to it at any time. Guest accounts can be deleted in the game, and unused guest accounts are deleted automatically after 12 months.

1. In the game

  1. Open Settings and scroll to the bottom.
  2. Tap Delete account, read the warning and tap Request deletion.

2. By email (Google- or Apple-linked accounts)

  1. Send an email to contact@cricketwarz.com with the subject "Cricketwarz account deletion request".
  2. If your account is linked to Google or Apple, send the email from the same email address as the linked account and include your nickname and account number (shown at the bottom of the game settings, for example #1234). We delete a linked account by email only when the sender address matches the linked account.
  3. Guest accounts (not linked to Google or Apple) cannot be confirmed by email, so we cannot delete them from an email request. Please delete a guest account in the game: Settings > Delete account. If you have removed the game, a guest account that is not used for 12 months is deleted automatically, including any purchased items.
  4. We verify the request, delete the account within 30 days and reply to you by email when it is done.

What happens next

After your request there is a 7-day grace period. During this time you cannot play, but you can open the game and tap Cancel deletion to keep your account. Signing in again does not cancel the request. After 7 days your account and the data below are permanently deleted and cannot be restored. Email requests are completed within 30 days of your request.

Data that is deleted

  • Account: guest account identifier, account number, nickname, login records (including the device identifier) and any linked Google or Apple account (including its email address). If you linked Apple, we also revoke the Sign in with Apple token.
  • Game data: coins and gems, characters with their levels, stars and shards, batting order, match records and results, battle points, club membership, join requests and contributions (including past seasons), mailbox items, attendance and ad-chest counters
  • Club: if you are a club Captain, the captaincy passes to a Vice-captain or, if there is none, to the longest-standing member. If you are the only member, the club is closed.

Data kept after deletion

  • Records of purchases are kept separately for 5 years from the date of purchase, as required by Korea's Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, and then deleted. They contain only the order number, product, the number of paid gems bought and used, and the account number, which can no longer be linked to you once your account is deleted.
  • Crash reports are kept without your account number, so they no longer show who sent them.
  • A scrambled (hashed) value of a linked Google or Apple account, so that the account-linking reward cannot be claimed twice. It cannot be turned back into your Google or Apple account details.
  • Records of actions taken by our support staff (such as corrections or suspensions), for as long as needed for security and handling disputes.
  • Server logs (including IP addresses) are deleted automatically after 90 days.

Before you delete

Unused paid in-game currency (such as gems) and remaining premium pass days are not refunded when an account is deleted. If you want a refund for a purchase, please contact Google Play or us before deleting your account.
