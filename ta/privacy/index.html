3. Advertising

Cricketwarz offers optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. An ad plays only when you tap a button to watch one for a reward; you can play the game without watching any ads.

Google AdMob may collect and process your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, device information and ad interactions under Google's Privacy Policy. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we ask for your consent through Google's consent tool (User Messaging Platform) before personalized ads are shown. You receive the same rewards whether or not you allow personalized ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. On iOS, the app asks for your permission through App Tracking Transparency when you first start it, and AdMob uses your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow it. If you decline, you receive the same rewards and see non-personalized ads. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Ad measurement (Google Firebase Analytics): To measure whether an ad led to installing and playing the game, the Android and iOS apps use Google Firebase Analytics. It sends app-usage events such as first open, sessions, finished matches (game mode, ground and result), in-app purchases and ad impressions, together with the Firebase app instance ID, device model, operating system, app version and an approximate country/region derived from your IP address, to Google. Your advertising ID is used for ad measurement only where allowed: in regions that require consent (such as the EU and the UK) only according to your choice on the consent screen. We do not send your name, email address, nickname or game account number. Processing follows the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy information. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings; on iOS, the advertising identifier is used only if you allow tracking, which you can turn off in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Analytics data is collected only after you agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy when you first start the game. You can turn it off at any time in Settings > Send analytics; once it is off, the app stops sending analytics data from your device. If you request account deletion, analytics collection also stops and the analytics ID on your device is reset.

4. In-app purchases

Cricketwarz offers a premium pass (30 days per purchase; up to 90 days can be held at a time; it does not renew automatically), gem packs and a one-time growth pack as in-app purchases. Payments are processed by Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iOS) under their own terms and privacy policies. We receive only the information needed to verify and deliver your purchase (purchase token or transaction ID, order ID, product, time of purchase and store country), never your card or other payment details.

So that a purchase is delivered to the right game account, the game gives the store a scrambled (hashed) form of your account number. It does not contain your name or contact details. If a purchase is refunded, cancelled or charged back, we take back what it granted, as described in our Terms of Service.

5. Website

This website is a static information site. We do not ask you to create an account and we do not set cookies.

To understand how the site is used, your browser may send our server the address of each page you open, the kind of main button you press (for example, a store button or an email link), and, if you arrive from another site, the kind of site you came from. These are counted only as daily totals. The page counter does not store IP addresses, cookies or device information.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), whose servers may record IP addresses in their logs for security purposes.

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as needed to operate the service:

  • Other players can see your nickname, your characters and match results during and after a match, and, if you join a club, your nickname, level, role and contribution in that club.
  • Service providers who process information on our behalf (section 7).
  • Google, for app distribution, payments and purchase verification (Google Play) for advertising (AdMob) and for ad measurement (Firebase Analytics), as described in sections 3 and 4.
  • Authorities, when required by applicable law.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections between the game and our servers and restricted access to our database. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal information, to receive a copy of it, and to withdraw consent at any time. To make a request, email us with your nickname and account number (shown in the game settings). To protect your account, we act on requests for an account linked to Google or Apple only when the email comes from the linked address; a guest account can be managed and deleted only in the game (Settings > Delete account). We respond without undue delay, and within one month where the GDPR applies. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Users in India: under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask for a summary of the personal data we process about you, ask us to correct, complete, update or erase it, and nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of your death or incapacity. You can raise any grievance with our grievance officer (section 14) at contact@cricketwarz.com; we acknowledge it within 48 hours and resolve it within one month. If you are not satisfied with our reply, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

11. Children

Cricketwarz is not directed at children under 13. If the law of your country requires the consent of a parent or guardian for a minor to use online services or make purchases, please use Cricketwarz only with that consent. If you believe a child has given us personal information without the required consent, please contact us and we will delete it.

12. International transfers

Our game servers and database are located in the Republic of Korea. If you use Cricketwarz from outside Korea, your information is transferred to and processed in Korea. For users in the European Union, the European Commission has recognized Korea as providing an adequate level of data protection (adequacy decision of December 17, 2021). Google, Cloudflare and GitHub may process information in other countries, including the United States, under their own safeguards.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the service or the law changes (for example, when we add or change features). Each version is identified by the effective date at the top of this page. We will announce significant changes in the game or on this website before they take effect.
